Passing & Defense Breakdown

Learn to build a solid forearm platform, move efficiently, and read opponents.

Platform Setup & Mechanics

  • Locked Elbows: Extend both arms straight out, clasping one hand inside the other with thumbs aligned side-by-side. Never cross your thumbs.
  • Angle Over Swing: Do not swing your arms at the ball. Angle your platform toward the center of the court where your setter is positioned.

Footwork & Positioning

  • Base Stance: Feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, knees bent, weight on the balls of your feet, and hips facing the incoming ball.
  • Shuffle Step: Move laterally using quick shuffle steps rather than crossing your feet to maintain balance and readiness.

Reading the Hitter

Keep your eyes on the opponent's hitting shoulder. If the shoulder drops or slows down, prepare for a tip or short free ball. Adjust your platform body angle before contact to push the ball up into the target zone.

Serving & Setting Technique

Master consistent float serves and soft setting hands.

Standing Float Serve

  1. The Stance: Step forward with your non-dominant foot, keeping your chest open toward your target zone.
  2. The Toss: Lift the ball 1-2 feet above your head in a controlled, non-spinning motion with your non-dominant hand.
  3. The Contact: Strike the center of the ball flat with the palm of your hand, stopping right after contact to kill spin and create unpredictable float.

Setting Hand Touch

  1. Forming the Window: Shape your hands into a soft triangle or basket shape right above your forehead, spreading fingers wide.
  2. Contact & Extension: Catch and release the ball in one fluid motion, using both wrist cushion and leg extension.
  3. Avoiding Violations: Never let the ball rest in your palms or drag below your eye line to prevent double-contact and lift calls.

Hitting Mechanics & Approach

Build explosive jump height, arm swing speed, and safe landing habits.

Standard 3-Step Approach (Right-Handed)

Step 1 (Left)

Directional step to trace the setter's ball trajectory.

Step 2 (Right)

Long, fast plant step converting speed into vertical power.

Step 3 (Left)

Closing step bringing feet parallel to explode upward into the jump.

Arm Swing & Shoulder Safety

Pull your hitting elbow high and back behind your ear while pointing your non-dominant hand at the ball ("Bow and Arrow" stance). Reach for maximum height, strike the ball at full arm extension, and snap your wrist downward to generate topspin. Always warm up your rotator cuffs prior to attacking drills!

Court IQ, Rotations & Rules

Eliminate rotation overlap penalties and communicate clearly during rallies.

Indoor Rotations & Overlaps

Teams rotate clockwise whenever winning back the right to serve (a side-out). Before the server contacts the ball, each player must remain strictly behind or beside their positional court teammates to avoid illegal overlap penalties.

Essential Court Callouts

  • "MINE!": Call early and loudly to prevent collisions between defensive passers.
  • "FREE BALL!": Shouted when an opponent sends an easy ball over, allowing your team to set an offense.
  • "OUT!": Warns teammates not to touch a ball traveling past boundaries.
